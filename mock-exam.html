PL-300 Mock Exam

Questions are shuffled and feedback is hidden until you finish. The timer is set to 100 minutes to mirror the current official exam duration.

Study score, not official scaled score.

Your result here is a raw percentage. Microsoft certification results use scaled scoring, with a passing score of 700. Do not treat 70% here as a guaranteed pass-equivalent.

Before you begin

  • Set aside uninterrupted time.
  • Choose the best Microsoft-style answer, not merely a technically possible answer.
  • Some questions use multiple select, Yes/No, matching, ordering or case-study formats.
  • No answer explanations appear until you submit.
